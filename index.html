Company Profile
01 — INTRODUCTION

End-to-end, under one umbrella

BUNA Contracting is a leading Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) company specialising in the delivery of high-quality building, infrastructure, and industrial projects. With a commitment to excellence, innovation and reliability, BUNA has established itself as a trusted partner for public and private sector clients across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the wider region.

The company provides end-to-end solutions covering design, engineering, procurement, construction and project management — spanning mixed-use developments, high-rise buildings, civil and infrastructure works, industrial facilities, commercial and residential developments, and institutional developments.
